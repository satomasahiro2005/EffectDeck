New since 2026.10.16

Undo
- Undo and Redo for the effect chain: parameter changes, adding, removing and moving effects. Shake your iPhone, swipe left or right with three fingers, or press ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z on a keyboard.

Follow Volume
- An effect can follow the output volume. Choose Follow Volume in the card's ⋯ menu, then add keyframes with the + on the strip under the card name.
- JSFX can read the output volume through effectdeck_output_volume.

Also new
- EffeTune 2.16.0 (DSP 0.16.0): Note Spectrogram Chords & Key, Rhythm Analyzer drums, 10 Mastering presets.
- EffeTune 2.15.0: SFZ Note Player Detection.
- Add Effect search groups results into Effects / Plugins / Presets and also finds common names (de-esser, goniometer, brir).
- An effect you add now goes outside Sections, unless you drop it onto a Section card.
- Chroma Spiral and Stereo Meter use the space better when collapsed. Note Spectrogram has an opaque keyboard, sharper notes and a legend readable in light mode.
- Floating Meters and graphs use less CPU.
- Accessibility: VoiceOver labels, larger text, standard buttons.

Fixes
- iPad (two columns): the top-right buttons could stop responding after closing a sheet by tapping outside it.
