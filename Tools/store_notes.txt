New since 2026.10.08

Remote Control
- Edit the effect chain of EffeTune 2.14 on a computer on the same network. Allow remote control in EffeTune's settings, then scan its QR code from Settings → Remote. IR Reverb can choose from the computer's IR Library.

iPad
- Cards with a graph switch between open and graph only when tapped, in two columns too.
- Rotating keeps your place in the chain. Cards were misaligned after rotating until you scrolled.

Dragging
- A card you drag folds to its title until you drop it, so it is easy to move past other cards.
- Dragging is smoother.

Floating Meters
- Spectrogram, waveform, spectrum, stereo and level meters of the chain input or output in a Picture in Picture window, over any app. Turn it on from the … menu → Floating Meters.

Also new
- EffeTune 2.14.0 (DSP 0.14.0): Analog Meter Custom mode, Oscilloscope up to 10 seconds (smooth scrolling with Trigger Mode Off), Note Spectrogram instrument colours, Highlight and onsets.
- SFZ Note Player plays SFZ instruments you import from Files, keeps them in the SFZ Library, and plays a note when you touch its voice display.
- Adaptive Prediction, Cassette Mode and the new Rhythm Analyzer layout (EffeTune 2.13.0).
- IR Reverb shows the energy decay curve and RT60 of the loaded impulse response.
- Settings → About → Reset All Data erases chains, presets, imported files and settings, including the iCloud copy.
- Share this chain always makes an effectdeck.nemut.ai link that opens in EffectDeck.
- Less work while idle: no 96/192 kHz resampling while bypassed, and the extension and the app wake only when audio arrives.

Fixes
- Remote Control: the Scan QR Code sheet closed immediately.

Previous updates (2026.10.08):

- Output Correction: assign one of your presets to each pair of headphones or speaker. It is applied after the chain and switches automatically when the output changes.
- Third-party AUv3 plug-ins appear under Plugins, and Apple Audio Units show their own interface.
- JSFX scripts run about 1.3 to 1.4 times faster on iPhone.
